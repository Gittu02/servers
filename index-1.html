💰 Economy & Balance 6 Commands
/bal
» /bal
Check your current account balance. Add a player name to check someone else's balance.
Economy
/baltop
» /baltop
View the top richest players on the server. Compete for the #1 spot on the wealth leaderboard.
Economy
/pay <player> <amount>
» /pay Steve 500
Send money to another player. Used for trades, services, or splitting profits with partners.
Economy
/eco give <player> <amount>
» /eco give Steve 1000
Admin command to give money to a player. Used for rewards, events, or starter kits.
Economy
/withdraw <amount>
» /withdraw 1000
Withdraw money as a physical bank note item you can trade or store in your inventory.
Economy
/deposit <amount>
» /deposit all
Deposit physical bank note items back into your account. Use "all" to deposit everything at once.
Economy
🏪 Shop & Selling 7 Commands
/shop
» /shop
Open the server's Admin Shop GUI where you can buy and sell items at fixed prices set by staff.
Shop
/sellgui
» /sellgui
Open the sell GUI to quickly drag and drop your items for instant cash. Fastest way to sell your loot.
Shop
/sell hand
» /sell hand
Sell the item currently in your hand to the server shop instantly without opening any GUI.
Shop
/sell all
» /sell all
Sell all sellable items in your entire inventory at once. Perfect after mining or farming runs.
Shop
/buy <item> [amount]
» /buy diamond 64
Buy items from the server shop by name directly from chat. Specify amount for bulk purchases.
Shop
/pshop create <price>
» /pshop create 50
Create a personal player shop chest by looking at a chest. Set your own prices for any item you want to sell.
Shop
/pshop list
» /pshop list
View all of your active player shops, their items, prices, and current stock levels.
Shop

» /ah
Open the global Auction House GUI to browse all active player listings. Find rare items and great deals.
Auction

List the item in your hand on the Auction House at your desired price. Other players can buy it instantly.
Auction
/ah search <item>
» /ah search netherite
Search the Auction House for specific items by name. Quickly find what you're looking to buy.
Auction
/ah listings
» /ah listings
View all of your current active listings on the Auction House. Manage prices and remove items.
Auction
/ah expired
» /ah expired
Collect your unsold items and money from expired or cancelled auction listings. Don't forget to check!
Auction
/ah player <name>
» /ah player Steve
Browse all active auction listings from a specific player. Useful for finding a trader's full stock.
Auction

🏗️ Land Claims & Protection 6 Commands
/claim
» /claim
Claim the chunk you are standing in. Protect your builds, shops, and valuables from other players.
Land
/unclaim
» /unclaim
Remove the land claim from the chunk you are standing in. Refunds your claim blocks.
Land
/claimslist
» /claimslist
View all of your claimed land areas, their coordinates, and remaining claim blocks balance.
Land
/trust <player>
» /trust Steve
Give another player full trust in your claimed land — they can build, break, and open chests.
Land
/accesstrust <player>
» /accesstrust Steve
Allow a player to open doors, buttons, chests, and use switches in your claim — but not build or break.
Land
/untrust <player>
» /untrust Steve
Revoke all trust from a player in your claimed land. They will no longer have any permissions.
Land
